# Blood and Olive Oil
Blood and Olive Oil is a 2d murder mystery set in 1889. Play as an experienced detective interrogating furry (and feathery) guests. Solve the clues to uncover the culprit, and TRUST NO ONE.

## Features
- Visual novel gameplay
- Fully animated CG cutscenes
- Both fast-paced and slow-paced minigames
- A cast of diverse characters
- Moderately historically accurate (We would like to apologize to our US History teacher in advance for this slight inconvenience.)

## Setting (History lesson for the nerds)
Blood and olive oil takes place during the Industrial Revolution, but more specifically the Gilded age. During this time, many got rich off of coal and the expansion of labor. These people were often known as "Robber Barrons." Strahd is one of these upperclassmen, but chose to utilize the power of olive oil (which he discovered on an expedition to the Mediterranian long ago) as the prime driver of his business. Either way, these lavishly rich people often owned extravagent "summer cottages" in the US State of Rhode Island, where Strahd plans to retire.

## The Cast
Officer - The character you play as. He is dedicated, serious, and trying to crack the case. (Crow)
Eren Barnes - The sidekick you just hired. He is nerdy, emotional, but relatively inexperienced. (Pigeon)

Carmen Strahd - Lavish millionare who pioneered the usage of olive oil during the industrial revolution. He is reserved, charming, but known to keep his sectets (Cat).

Mason Strahd - Carmen's spoiled son. He is lazy, egocentric, and obsessed with the latest fashion. (Cat)

Ethel Crosswire - As Carmen's head maid of over 20 years, she's been through both the ups and downs of the Strahd family business. She is strict, meticulous, and clinically underpaid. (Ostrich)

Raegan Williams - Carmen's childhood best friend and business partner. Raegan is loyal, but he doesn't like to take risks. (Lion)

Elizabeth Williams - While she is Raegan's wife, she's known to flirt with other guys so they can spill intel, which she uses to bolster her husband's business. Elizabeth is flirty, crass, and sly. (Hyena)

Barron Rogers - Barron is THE defenition of "by hook or by crook." He sees the industry as a game, and him as the winner. He's made deals with the likes of the Vanderbilts and J.P. Morgan. Barron is manipulative, cunning, and competitive. (Salamander)

The Olive Man - While Strahd may not recognize him today, they had a fierce competition in the Mediterranian for who could access the best olive oil. After *an incident*, the guy known only as "The Olive Man" wishes for revenge. He is petty, outlandish, and believes in the supernatural. (Human)
